# **Food Hub Rosters**

The Food Hub calendar is where you put up shifts and volunteers sign up for the ones they want. This guide is for Food Hub admins running the rosters.

You do everything from the **Food Hub group** on the website — its tabs (Shifts, Manage Members, and so on). You never need to go into any admin or settings area.

> **New to this?** If you can’t see the things this guide describes, your access probably hasn’t been set up yet — just ask a site administrator to set you up as a Food Hub admin. To set someone *else* up, see [Setting Up Food Hub People](https://tusa-dev.its.utas.edu.au/docs/tusa-operations-guide/food-hub/food-hub-rosters/#setting-up-food-hub-people).

---



## **Adding and removing volunteers**



Adding a volunteer to the hub is covered in [Setting Up Food Hub People](https://tusa-dev.its.utas.edu.au/docs/tusa-operations-guide/food-hub/food-hub-rosters/#setting-up-food-hub-people) — you add them to the Food Hub group, then put them on a calendar.

**To remove someone** who’s stopped volunteering, take them out of the Food Hub group:

- Go to the Food Hub group and open the **Manage Members** tab.
- Find the person and remove them.

That automatically takes them off all their Food Hub calendars and out of the volunteer list, so they can’t be scheduled anymore. You don’t need to do anything separately in the shift system.

---



## **Putting up shifts**



New shifts start as a **draft**. A draft shift is only visible to you, not to the volunteers yet. Each shift shows a small icon for whether it’s a draft or published (a question mark means it’s still a draft).

When you’re ready for volunteers to sign up, **publish** the shift. As soon as it’s published, it shows up for them to pick.

So the flow is simple: build the shift, then publish it when you want people to be able to grab it.

---



## **Seeing who’s coming**



On the Food Hub group you can see the shifts for your hub and who has signed up for each one, so you can plan who’s doing what on the day.

---



## **If something looks wrong**



- **A shift you set up isn’t showing for volunteers.** Check that you’ve **published** it, and not left it as a draft.
- **A volunteer can’t pick up shifts even though you added them.** They’ve been added to the group but probably haven’t been put on a calendar yet. Add them to the right Food Hub calendar (Calendar column → Edit), and check the shifts are published.
- **You can’t see the roster at all.** Your access probably hasn’t been set up. Ask a site administrator to set you up as a Food Hub admin.

# Welcome to the TUSA Operations Guide

Welcome to the TUSA IT team! This guide is designed to help you get up to speed quickly, manage your day-to-day responsibilities, and find technical resources as an IT intern.

## What you will find here:

- **Getting Started:** Setting up your local workspace, tools, and access credentials.
- **Core Workflows:** Daily routines, standups, and how we handle Jira ticketing.
- **Technical Guides:** Deployment paths and troubleshooting documentation.
- **Resources:** Important team contact lists and emergency channels.

Use the sidebar navigation to explore the documentation, or use the search bar at the top to find specific topics.

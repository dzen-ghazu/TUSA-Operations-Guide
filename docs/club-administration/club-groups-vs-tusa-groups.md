# **Club Groups vs TUSA Groups**

There are two kinds of BuddyBoss group on the site, and it helps to know which one you are looking at:

- **Club groups** — one for each affiliated club.
- **TUSA groups** — used for TUSA’s own entities, such as Food Hub, Student Council, and the staff-only TUSA Internal group.

They are built on the same technology and look similar, which is why they are easy to mix up. The differences are in how they are set up and how people get into them.



## **Club groups**



A **club group** is a club’s own community space. It holds that club’s members, its store, its events, and its resources. Every affiliated club has one.

Club groups are **uniform**. They are all set up the same way, with dynamic content that fills itself in for each club, so a new club gets a working group without anyone building it by hand.

Membership of a club group is **automatic**. When someone buys a membership, that purchase applies a tag, and the tag is what lets them into the club group. During affiliation, the system also adds the club’s committee to their group automatically. People come and go often, and the system handles it.



## **TUSA groups**



A **TUSA group** is for one of TUSA’s own entities rather than a club. Food Hub, Student Council, and the TUSA Internal group are all examples. They are not clubs and they do not go through the club affiliation process.

TUSA groups use **almost the same** dynamic setup as clubs, but each one tends to be a little different and needs its own configuration and thought. They are not a one-size-fits-all template the way clubs are, so setting one up is more hands-on.

Membership of a TUSA group is **manual**. There are no membership tags or purchases that put someone into a TUSA group, and nothing adds people automatically. People are added by hand, as part of onboarding, and it happens far less often than with clubs. Adding or removing someone is a deliberate step, not something the system does on its own.



## **The TUSA Internal group is one of these**



The **TUSA Internal group** is a TUSA group, and it is the staff control room. It is where TUSA staff run the organisation behind the scenes: reviewing club applications, processing grants, checking profile claims, finding club documents, handling incident reports, and so on. There is only one of it, only staff use it, and like every TUSA group, people are added to it by hand.

If you want the detail on what lives inside the TUSA Internal group, that is its own topic. The point here is simply that it is a **TUSA group, not a club** — which is also why it does not behave like one.



## **The short version**



- Two group types: **club groups** (the clubs) and **TUSA groups** (TUSA’s entities, including TUSA Internal).
- **Clubs:** uniform setup, automatic membership (a membership purchase tags you in).
- **TUSA groups:** bespoke setup, **manual** membership — no tags, no auto-add, people are onboarded by hand.
- The TUSA Internal group is a TUSA group, which is why it is not, and does not work like, a club.

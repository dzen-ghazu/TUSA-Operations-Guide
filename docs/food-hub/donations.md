# **Food Hub Donations: Where the Records Are Kept**

People can donate to the Food Hub through the website at **/donate**. This guide is for TUSA staff who need to find those donations, report on them, or answer a question about one.

**The short version: donation records live in Stripe, not on the website.**

Stripe holds the payment, the donor’s name and email, the amount and the date. That is the record you want. The website is not where you look for a list of donations.

> **Why not on the website?** Stripe already holds everything, properly and securely, and it is the system TUSA uses for every other payment. Keeping a second copy of people’s donation details on the website would mean holding personal information we do not need.



## **Finding donations in Stripe**



Log in to the TUSA Stripe account and go to **Payments**.

Food Hub donations are identifiable three ways:

- **The description** reads *Products: Food Hub Donation*, so you can spot them while scrolling the payments list.
- `donation_type` is recorded on every one as *Food Hub Donation*.
- `donation_tier` records which amount they chose, for example *1 week of food for 2 x students*, or *Another amount I choose* if they entered their own.

Those last two are metadata. You can **search and filter on them in Stripe**, and they come through in CSV exports.

That is what makes real reporting possible. Because the tier is recorded, you can report on what the donations actually funded, not just a dollar total. “This year’s donations funded 340 student-weeks of food” is a more useful sentence than “we raised $3,400”, and both come from the same export.

> **Worth knowing:** the tier is only recorded on donations made from August 2026 onwards. Anything earlier will not have it.



## **What the donor receives**



Two emails, doing different jobs:

- **A receipt from Stripe**, with the amount, date and payment reference. This is the record of their payment.
- **A thank you from TUSA**, sent from the Food Hub, with no payment details in it at all.

That second one has no amount or date on purpose. If both emails carried the figures and one was ever wrong, the donor would have two documents disagreeing about what they paid. Stripe’s is the one that counts.

They also see a confirmation on screen straight after donating.



## **Important: donations are not tax deductible**



TUSA does not hold DGR status, so **donations made through the website cannot be claimed as a tax deduction**. Nothing in the website confirmation or the thank you email says or implies otherwise, and it needs to stay that way.

This is why the MyCause campaign still exists separately.

**If someone asks about tax deductibility, do not answer from this guide.** Pass it to the TUSA General Manager.



## **The Stripe account**



Donations go to the **same Stripe account** TUSA uses for club stores and everything else on the website. There is not a separate donations account.

That matters if anyone is ever asked to change Stripe settings or keys, because a change made for one part of the site affects all of it.



## **Who to ask**



For a question about a specific donation, contact the Food Hub team at **[Food.Hub@tusa.utas.edu.au](mailto:Food.Hub@tusa.utas.edu.au)**.

For access to the Stripe account, speak to whoever administers TUSA’s Stripe. That is not the website team.

# **Club Membership Numbers and Reports**

Sometimes you need to know how many members a club has, or a full breakdown of who is in it. The site has two reports for this, and both download as a spreadsheet you can open in Excel.

## **Why the Members tab does not show a number**

On a club dashboard, **Manage > Members** shows the people in the club as a list of profiles. It is built for browsing who is there, not for counting them, so it does not show a total. For a figure, use one of the reports below.

## **One club at a time**

From a club’s own dashboard there is a **Download Member List (CSV)** button. For that club it gives you:

- the total number of members,
- how many are organisers, moderators and regular members,
- how many are current UTAS students, and the percentage,
- the mix of profile types (student, alumni, community member),
- and the full member list, with names, roles and emails.

## **Every club at once**

For the whole picture across all clubs, go to the **TUSA Internal group > Functions > CSV** and use **Download All Clubs (CSV)**. It gives you:

- an overall summary: total clubs, total members across all of them, and the overall student percentage,
- a line for every club, with each club’s total members, role breakdown and student percentage,
- and a detailed list of every member across every club.

This is the one to reach for when you need membership numbers for the whole clubs program, or a figure for something like the C&S Awards.

## **Good to know** 

- Both reports are a **download**, not a live page. You open the file in Excel.
- The numbers are read from the site at the moment you download, so they are current as of then.
- The all-clubs report is staff only. The single-club one is available to that club’s own admins as well.
